Where silent giants pierce the sky,
And ancient winds go sweeping by,
A different thunder wakes the ground,
Where cricket’s heartbeat can be found.

From dusty alleys, brick and stone,
To packed arenas newly grown,
With taped-up tennis balls they played,
Where dreams of glory first were made.

In Kirtipur, the banners rise,
Beneath the wide Himalayan skies,
A sea of blue, a roaring crowd,
Whose passion echoes, fierce and proud.

The Rhinos charge with fearless heart,
Prepared to play a masterclass part,
With willow bats and eyes of fire,
They push the boundaries higher, higher.

The bowler runs, the leather flies,
A tracer bullet to the skies,
The stand erupts in wild acclaim,
As legends script a deathless name.

Through uphill battles, fierce and long,
They built a nation’s battle song,
From humble pitches, rough and bare,
To global stages, fighting fair.

No billion-dollar wealth they hold,
Just burning grit and hearts of gold,
A story written stroke by stroke,
Where slumbering giants softly woke.

The spinners weave a magic spell,
While fiery paces break the shell,
In every yorker, every drive,
A nation's hopes come fast alive.

From Kathmandu to far-off shores,
The white ball swings, the willow roars,
The young and old unite as one,
To watch the rising eastern sun.

They stand against the cricket kings,
On wings of hope that courage brings,
Unfazed by rank, untamed by fear,
They make the world stand up and hear.

Let flags of crimson flutter high,
Where eagles wheel and falcons fly,
For every six that clears the rope,
Carries a million dreams of hope.

So let the mighty stadium cheer,
For cricket’s true cathedral here—
A people's joy, a sacred flame,
Forever bound to Nepal's name.
